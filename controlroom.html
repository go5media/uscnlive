Inside Our Control Room

How We Monitor Your Property

At USCN Live, our control room are designed to help operators monitor CCTV feeds, investigate suspicious activity and respond to potential security incidents.

Our monitoring workstations use multiple displays, allowing operators to focus on selected cameras while maintaining wider visual awareness through additional screens and video walls.

When something requires closer attention, operators can bring the relevant camera feed onto their main display for further investigation. Where supported, they can also remotely adjust compatible cameras using dedicated controls.

This approach combines focused human observation with the flexibility to investigate activity across multiple monitored locations.
