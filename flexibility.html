Flexible Monitoring, Fair Pricing

Monitoring That Works for You

At USCN Live, we believe you should only have to pay for the monitoring you actually need.

Whether you're going on holiday, protecting your business during a busy period or looking for long-term security, we offer flexible monitoring agreements ranging from just seven days to two years.

We don't believe everyone should be forced into a lengthy contract simply to access professional CCTV monitoring.

Why We Offer Flexible Plans

Every customer has different security requirements. Some need temporary protection, while others want the reassurance of ongoing monitoring.

Our flexible approach allows you to choose a monitoring period that suits your circumstances.

Longer agreements also help us plan our operations and reduce the costs associated with finding and onboarding new customers. We recognise the value of that commitment by offering more favourable rates for longer-term customers.

Keeping Monitoring Affordable

We use economies of scale to help keep our monitoring services affordable.

By monitoring CCTV systems for multiple customers through shared control-room facilities, we can spread the costs of our operators, equipment and infrastructure across the service.

This allows us to offer monitoring at more accessible prices while maintaining our focus on service quality.

More Cameras, Better Value

Once your CCTV system is connected to our monitoring service, adding further cameras can be more economical than setting up an entirely new customer.

We reflect these efficiencies in our pricing, allowing customers with multiple cameras to benefit from reduced per-camera monitoring rates.

Having several camera views can also help our operators gain a clearer understanding of activity around your property when investigating potential security incidents.

Flexible Payment Options

We understand that paying for an entire monitoring agreement upfront isn't always practical.

That's why we offer eligible customers the choice between monthly instalments and paying for their agreement in full.

Upfront payments help us invest in our operations and future development, so we offer an additional saving to customers who choose this option. However, we also recognise the importance of manageable monthly payments.

Security With a Community Focus

USCN Live is part of UK Safety Camera Network Ltd, an organisation focused on security and public safety.

Our aim isn't simply to maximise profits. We want to make professional CCTV monitoring more accessible to homeowners, businesses and communities while operating a financially sustainable service.

We believe effective security shouldn't be reserved only for those with the largest budgets.

Starting Small, Planning Ahead

Our initial monitoring service operates from 6:00 PM to 1:00 AM, seven days a week, including weekends and bank holidays.

By initially concentrating on these hours, we can manage our operating costs while establishing demand for the service.

As USCN Live grows, we intend to explore longer monitoring periods, with the ambition of eventually offering 24-hour monitoring.

Our approach is simple: provide a practical, affordable service today while developing the capacity to serve more customers in the future.
