Live Alerts at USCN LIVE

What are they?

Live Alerts are triggered when a live camera operator see a crime being committed.

They can contact the emergency services on your behalf without needing any action from you.

Depending on our communication agreement with you (which explains what you wish USCN Live to inform you of) an operator may also contact you directly.

What does Alerts Cost

Live Alerts come as standard with any CCTV Monitoring packages, you do not need to pay any extra on top, as we understand that live CCTV is not cheap.
