About Us

What is USCN Live?
USCN Live is a CCTV monitoring service operated by UK Safety Camera Network Ltd. We help homeowners, businesses and property managers protect their premises through live remote CCTV monitoring.

Why we do it
We believe reliable CCTV monitoring should be accessible to everyone, from individual homeowners to large businesses. Our aim is to make professional monitoring straightforward, affordable and dependable.

Who we work with?
We work with shops, offices, warehouses, restaurants, homeowners, landlords, apartment buildings and other residential and commercial premises across the UK.

Your Questions, Answered.

Is this a 24-hour monitoring service?

No. USCN Live offers scheduled monitoring during agreed evening hours, with available windows between 6 pm and 1 am.

How are the discounts calculated?

The selected monitoring-hours tariff determines the base hourly rate. Each additional camera reduces the hourly rate for all cameras by 5p, capped at a 95p reduction. The contract loyalty discount is then applied to the resulting monitoring charge.

Can I request monitoring for more than 20 cameras?

Yes. The automatic quantity discount stops increasing at 20 cameras, but the calculator supports up to 40 cameras. Actual capacity and arrangements must be confirmed.

The service

Focused monitoring during an agreed evening window.

USCN Live monitors your existing CCTV cameras for your own premises during agreed evening hours. For interconnected coverage across multiple shops and streets, explore our separate High Watch programme.

01 / SCHEDULE

Choose your monitoring hours

Select two, four, six or seven hours per night, within the available 6 pm to 1 am window.

02 / CAMERAS

Specify the views to monitor

Configure the number of cameras. Additional cameras reduce the applicable per-camera hourly rate, up to the stated discount cap.

03 / AGREEMENT

Confirm the arrangements

Monitoring, incident escalation, availability, dates and contractual terms are confirmed for the individual premises before service starts.
